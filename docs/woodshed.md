# Woodshed: the founders' rules email (2016)

**This is a primary source, preserved verbatim.** It is the rules email from
the original cfbpickem.net league — the document that let the founders' game
be recovered and rebuilt as `ContestMode::Woodshed` in August 2026. The 2016
CODE that sat beside it is gone; this text is what survived, so it is kept
here unaltered rather than left to a hard drive.

Read it as history, not as spec. What v4 actually implements — and what it
deliberately left in 2016 (the money, the divisions, the playoff, the single
Saturday deadline) — is [game-modes.md](game-modes.md). Where the two
disagree, game-modes.md is the app and this is the ancestor.

The email's text begins below the rule and has not been edited.

---

# Dates & Cycles
The regular season will start with College Football's Week 2 and run through Week 13. The first set of games will be made available by Thursday 9/6 with picks due Saturday 9/8 by NOON ET. The weeks "turn over" on Tuesdays and follow the same cycle with games available on Thursdays and due by noon on Saturdays. Therefore, games played during the week with a kick-off before Saturday at noon are ineligible for picks. Leagues close/lock at the start of games in Week 4 (players can join up until picks are due that week).

# Rules & Scoring
Each week, players submit picks against the spread for 15 total games. Those games are split into 3 tiers.

Tier 1 Games: 8 points
Tier 2 Games: 6 points
Tier 3 Games: 4 points

Better games will land in higher tiers.

### Locks
A featured game each week is eligible for a Lock option that allows the player to wager additional points. If the member picks incorrectly, points are deducted.

Lock Bonus: 6 points
Lock Penalty: -4 points

So...
Correct Locks are worth 14 points
Incorrect Locks are worth -4 points

Again, wagering the extra points is optional - meaning that you can simply treat the game as a normal Tier 1 game with point values of 0 or 8 points.

### The Bear
Each week, players can earn 5 additional points for “beating The Bear”. The Bear is a mythical contestant who makes celebrity picks each week that follow a theme, such as "All underdogs" or "School with the more fierce mascot". The Bear is ineligible for Lock options. You either beat the Bear (and receive 5 bonus points) or you don’t.

### Weekly Payouts
Players are placed in divisions, where the number of divisions depends on the player count each season.

Overall winner: Highest weekly point total, regardless of division: $70

Division Winners: Highest weekly point total in each division, excluding the overall winner's division: $35

Wild Card: Highest weekly point total where the player did not win their division (or win overall): $35

### Tiebreakers
When players submit picks, they submit an over/under value for the featured game of the week. The first tiebreaker is the lowest absolute value difference between their guess and the actual resulting over/under of the featured game. The second tiebreaker (rare) goes to the player who submitted or finished editing their picks earliest.

### Playoffs
As of now, the top 10 players (regardless of division) advance to the playoffs. That number is subject to change, depending on the final player count. Players missing the playoff cut can participate in a consolation bracket. There is a season intermission during the week of the annual Army/Navy game, as well as during conference championships. Bowl games are used for playoff pools. We'll reconvene when we get there.

Good Luck!
